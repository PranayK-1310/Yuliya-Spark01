01 — About

Born to move.
Trained to feel.

I'm Julia Spark — an actor, dancer, and choreographer based in Toronto, Ontario. Born with a restless spirit and a deep hunger for storytelling, I've spent my life finding ways to translate human experience through movement, voice, and presence.

My journey began in the studio — hours spent perfecting technique, pushing my body's limits, and learning that real artistry lives in the space between discipline and release. Dance gave me a vocabulary. Theatre gave me a voice. Together, they gave me a world.

I'm currently enrolled in the Acting for Film, TV and the Theatre program at Toronto Film School, where I most recently played Puck in the inaugural production of A Midsummer Night's…Romp! — Shakespeare reimagined with chaos, comedy, and an irreverent heart.

I believe in work that risks something. In characters who breathe and break. In the electricity between performers when everything is truly alive. That is what I bring to every role, every rehearsal, every frame.

02 — What I Do

01

Acting

Film, television, and theatre — I inhabit characters fully, building from the inside out. Trained in classical and contemporary technique, I bring emotional truth and physical precision to every performance.

02

Dance

Movement is my first language. From contemporary and jazz to physical theatre, I use the body as an instrument of storytelling — expressive, dynamic, and endlessly alive.

03

Choreography

I shape movement for stage and screen with an eye for composition, rhythm, and narrative clarity. My choreographic work is rooted in intention — every gesture earns its place in the story.

"I don't perform characters. I become them — every bruise, every joy, every contradiction."
— Julia Spark
